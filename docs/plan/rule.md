# War Chest — Game Rules

## Overview

- **Players:** 2 to 4
- **Goal:** Move your King to the Throne (cell J-10, center of the board), or kill the enemy King.
- Each player starts in a **3×7 homeland** along one edge of the board.

---

## Special Locations

| Location | Appearance | Effect |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| **Warp** | Black cells | When a unit enters, it may teleport to any other warp cell, or stay. |
| **Wall** | Thick border lines | Units cannot move or attack through a wall. Protects units entering the palace safely. |
| **Throne** | Gold cell (J-10) | King enters → win. Soldier enters → evolves into any unit (except King). Range unit enters → attack range +1, and can attack outside the wall without restriction. |

---

## Units

| Unit | Count | Type | Movement | Starting Position | Special |
|------|-------|------|----------|-------------------|---------|
| **King** | 1 | Melee | Any adjacent cell | Center of back row (e.g. J-1) | Entering the Throne wins the game. If killed, you lose. |
| **Guard** | 2 | Melee | Any adjacent cell | Either side of King (e.g. I-1, K-1) | Negates any ranged attack. Detects enemy Assassins within 2 cells. |
| **Assassin** | 2 | Range | 1–2 cells, any direction | Next to each Guard | Hidden until revealed. Revealed by killing a unit or moving within 2 cells of an enemy Guard. Can attack 1 cell in any direction without moving. |
| **Knight** | 2 | Melee | 1–4 cells, horizontal or vertical | Next to each Assassin | Can jump over 1 unit. Must move onto enemy cell to kill. |
| **Archer** | 4 | Range | Any adjacent cell | 2nd row, alternating (e.g. G-2, I-2, …) | Can attack 1–2 cells in any direction without moving. |
| **Artillery** | 2 | Range | 1 cell, horizontal or vertical | 2nd row between Archers (e.g. H-2, L-2) | Can attack 2–4 cells horizontally or vertically without moving. |
| **Magician** | 1 | Range | 1–2 cells, any direction | Center of 2nd row (e.g. J-2) | Can attack 1–3 cells in any direction without moving. Can hit 2 adjacent units at once. |
| **Soldier** | 7 | Melee | Any adjacent cell | Row 3 (e.g. G-3, H-3, …) | Evolves into any unit (except King) upon entering the Throne. |

---

## Combat Rules

- **Melee** units must move onto the enemy's cell to kill.
- **Range** units can attack without moving.
- Units cannot move or attack through a Wall.
