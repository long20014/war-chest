The game is playable for 2 to 4 players, each player start at 3x7 space (homeland) in 4 edge of the board. The goal of the game is to make the king access to the throne (the center cell of the board J-10) or kill enemy king.

Unit, move rule, start positon, special skill/attack:

- King (1 piece, melee): can move to any adjacent cell. Start at center of bottom row (eg: J-1). When move to center of the board (throne), you win. When king is killed, you lose.
- Guard (2 pieces, melee): can move to any adjacent cell. Start at 2 side next to the king  (eg: I-1, K-1). Can negate any range attack, can detect the present of enemy assassin within 2 cells.
- Assassin (2 pieces, range): can move 1 to 2 cells in any direction. Start next to the guard. can not be killed until being reveal, the assassin can only be revealed by killing any enemy unit or go near the guard within 2 cells. an attack 1 in any direct without moving. 
- Knight (2 pieces, melee): can move 1 to 4 cell in horizontal or vertical direction. Start next to assassin. Can jump over the head of 1 unit. (Melee unit require to move in the position the enemy to kill)
- Archer (4 pieces, range): can move to any adjacent cell. Start in 2nd row and stand alternating (e.g: G-2, I-2, so on). Can attack 1 to 2 cell in any direction without moving. (Range unit can attack without moving)
- Artillery (2 pieces, range): can move to 1 cell in horizontal or vertical direction. Start in 2nd row and stand at  H-2, L-2, the positon between two archer. Can attack 2 to 4 cell horizontal or vertical direction without moving.
- Magician (1 piece, range): can move 1 to 2 cells in any direction. Start in centerof 2nd row (e.g: J-2). Can attack 1 to 3 cell in any direction without moving. Can attack 2 unit at once if they stand next to each other.
- Soldier (7 piece, melee): can move to any adjacent cell. Start at row 3  (eg: G-3, H-3, so on). If it access to the throne, it can evolve to any type of unit you choose. (except king)
